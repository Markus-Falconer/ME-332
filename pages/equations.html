What Changed Since the Exam 1 Sheet

Added (pages 4–10 are new)
  • All of convection: dimensionless numbers, boundary layers and Chilton–Colburn, flat plate (constant $T_s$ and constant $q''$), unheated starting length, flow around objects, internal flow, free convection, mixed convection.
  • Heat exchangers: $1/UA$ with fouling and fins, the concentric-tube form, annulus $D_h$ and $A_c$, LMTD and ε–NTU definitions.
  • Finned surface resistance $R_{conv,o} = 1/(\eta_ohA_t)$.
Removed
  • The heat diffusion equation (Cartesian, cylindrical and spherical).
  • 2-D conduction: shape factors and the finite-difference approximations, including the transient (time-step) form.
  • One-term transient solutions for the infinite cylinder and sphere (only the plane wall is left).
  • The lumped-capacitance solution for constant $h$ with generation or applied flux (the $a$, $b$ form).
If any of the removed material shows up on Exam 2 you'd need it from memory, but the practice exam doesn't use any of it.

Typos on the Sheet

Where the printed sheet is wrong (correct versions are used everywhere on this site)
  • Laminar skin friction (p. 5): printed $C_{f,x} = 0.664Re_x^{1/2}$ and $\bar C_f = 1.328Re_x^{1/2}$. The exponent is $-1/2$: friction drops as the boundary layer grows.
  • Turbulent boundary layer thickness (p. 5): printed $\delta = 0.37xRe_x^{1/5}$. It's $0.37xRe_x^{-1/5}$.
  • Turbulent unheated starting length (p. 6): printed with the laminar exponents. The textbook form is $Nu_x = Nu_x|_{\xi=0}\big/[1 - (\xi/x)^{9/10}]^{1/9}$.
  • Mean temperature with an external fluid (p. 7): printed identical to the constant-$T_s$ line. It should read $\dfrac{T_\infty - T_m(x)}{T_\infty - T_{m,i}} = \exp\left(-\dfrac{\bar UA_s}{\dot mc_p}\right)$, with $T_\infty$ in place of $T_s$ and $\bar UA_s$ in place of $\bar hPx$.
  • Heat exchanger energy balance (p. 10): printed $\dot m_hc_{p,c}(T_{c,o} - T_{c,i})$. The cold side is $\dot m_cc_{p,c}(T_{c,o} - T_{c,i})$.
  • Lumped total heat (p. 3): printed $(\rho Vc_p)(T_i - T_\infty[1 - \exp(t/\tau)])$. It's $Q = (\rho Vc_p)(T_i - T_\infty)[1 - \exp(-t/\tau)]$.
  • Semi-infinite, constant flux (p. 4): the $q_s''(t)$ line under the constant-flux case is a copy of the constant-$T_s$ one. With constant flux, $q_s''$ is just the applied $q_o''$.
  • $g(Pr)$ (p. 9): printed $1.328Pr$; the textbook has $1.238Pr$. The difference in $\overline{Nu}$ is under 2%, so either is fine.
  • Energy conservation (p. 1): printed $\dot E_{gen} + \dot E_{in} + \dot E_{out}$. Read it as $\dot E_{in} - \dot E_{out} + \dot E_{gen}$.
If one of these matters for a problem, write the version you're using and a short note. Graders give credit for a stated, correct equation.

Not on the Sheet: Know These or Expect Them Given

You need these and they aren't printed
  • $\sigma = 5.67\times10^{-8}$ W/m²·K⁴ and $g = 9.81$ m/s².
  • Property temperature: film temperature $T_f = (T_s + T_\infty)/2$ for external flow and free convection (and $\beta = 1/T_f$ in kelvin). Mean temperature $\bar T_m = (T_{m,i} + T_{m,o})/2$ for internal flow.
  • Circular tube Reynolds number from mass flow: $Re_D = 4\dot m/(\pi D\mu)$ (from $\dot m = \rho u_mA_c$). For an annulus, use $Re = \dot mD_h/(A_c\mu)$, not the circular-tube shortcut.
  • Uniform $q_s''$ in a tube: $T_m(x) = T_{m,i} + \dfrac{q_s''P}{\dot mc_p}x$ and $T_s = T_m + q_s''/h$. Derive from $\dot mc_p\,dT_m = q_s''P\,dx$.
  • LMTD end differences: counterflow $\Delta T_1 = T_{h,i} - T_{c,o}$, $\Delta T_2 = T_{h,o} - T_{c,i}$; parallel flow $\Delta T_1 = T_{h,i} - T_{c,i}$, $\Delta T_2 = T_{h,o} - T_{c,o}$. The sheet only says "each side".
  • Counterflow with $C_r = 1$: the general ε formula is 0/0. Use $\varepsilon = NTU/(1 + NTU)$, or the fact that $\Delta T$ is constant along the exchanger.
Provided if needed ("see tables" / "other correlations provided as necessary")
  • $C$, $m$, $n$ for cylinders and other objects in cross flow (Hilpert, Zukauskas).
  • ε–NTU relations for specific arrangements. Copies are at the bottom of the heat exchanger section.
  • Annulus and noncircular duct $Nu$ tables (the practice exam supplied the annulus table).
  • Churchill–Bernstein, Churchill–Chu, Gnielinski, horizontal plate and cylinder free-convection correlations. They're on the chapter pages for HW, but the exam sheet doesn't have them.

General, Conduction and Transient (pp. 1–4)

Energy conservation and definitions p. 1
$$\frac{dE}{dt} = \dot E_{in} - \dot E_{out} + \dot E_{gen},\qquad q''= \frac{q}{A},\qquad \dot q = \frac{q_{gen}}{V},\qquad \alpha = \frac{k}{\rho c_p}$$
Fourier's law and 1-D walls (no generation) pp. 1–2
$$q = -kA\frac{dT}{dx},\qquad q_{plane} = \frac{kA(T_H - T_C)}{L}$$ $$q_{cyl} = \frac{2\pi kL(T_{s,1} - T_{s,2})}{\ln(r_2/r_1)},\qquad q_{sph} = \frac{4\pi k(T_{s,1} - T_{s,2})}{1/r_1 - 1/r_2}$$ $$\text{Plane wall: } T(x) = C_1x + C_2\ \ (\text{no gen}),\qquad T(x) = -\frac{\dot q}{2k}x^2 + C_1x + C_2\ \ (\text{with gen})$$
Thermal resistances p. 2
$$R_{plane} = \frac{L}{kA},\quad R_{cyl} = \frac{\ln(r_o/r_i)}{2\pi kL},\quad R_{sph} = \frac{1}{4\pi k}\left(\frac{1}{r_1} - \frac{1}{r_2}\right)$$ $$R_{conv} = \frac{1}{hA},\quad R_{conv,o} = \frac{1}{\eta_ohA_t},\quad R_{rad} = \frac{1}{h_rA},\quad R_{tc} = \frac{R''_{tc}}{A}$$ $$\text{Series: } R_{eq} = \sum R\qquad \text{Parallel: } \frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \sum\frac{1}{R_i}\qquad q = \frac{T_H - T_C}{\sum R}$$

Exam 2 use: the wall term in $1/UA$, and any problem where a pipe or duct also loses heat through insulation or to an outside flow.

Fins pp. 2–3
$$\epsilon_f = \frac{q_f}{hA_{c,b}\theta_b},\qquad \eta_f = \frac{q_f}{hA_f\theta_b},\qquad \eta_o = \frac{q_t}{hA_t\theta_b} = 1 - \frac{NA_f}{NA_f + A_b}(1 - \eta_f)$$

Exam 2 use: finned heat exchanger surfaces enter $1/UA$ as $1/(\eta_ohA)$. Check: $\eta_f \le \eta_o \le 1$.

Lumped capacitance ($Bi = hL_c/k \lt 0.1$, $L_c = V/A_s$) p. 3
$$\frac{T - T_\infty}{T_i - T_\infty} = \exp\left[-\left(\frac{hA_s}{\rho Vc_p}\right)t\right],\qquad \tau_t = \frac{\rho Vc_p}{hA_s}$$ $$t = -\frac{\rho Vc_p}{hA_s}\ln\frac{T - T_\infty}{T_i - T_\infty},\qquad Q = (\rho Vc_p)(T_i - T_\infty)\left[1 - \exp(-t/\tau_t)\right]$$ $$q_s''A_{s,h} + \dot E_g - hA_{s,c}(T - T_\infty) - \epsilon\sigma A_{s,r}(T^4 - T_{sur}^4) = \rho Vc_p\frac{dT}{dt}$$

Exam 2 use: get $h$ from a correlation (flat plate, cylinder, free convection), then plug it in here. Use the solid's $\rho$, $c_p$, $k$.

Plane wall, one-term solution ($Fo \gt 0.2$; half-thickness $L$, or full thickness if insulated at $x = 0$) p. 4
$$\theta^* = C_1\exp(-\zeta_1^2Fo)\cos(\zeta_1x^*),\quad \theta_o^* = C_1\exp(-\zeta_1^2Fo),\quad Fo = \frac{\alpha t}{L^2}$$ $$\frac{Q}{Q_o} = 1 - \frac{\sin\zeta_1}{\zeta_1}\theta_o^*,\quad Q_o = \rho Vc_p(T_i - T_\infty)$$
Semi-infinite solid p. 4
$$\text{Constant } T_s: \frac{T - T_s}{T_i - T_s} = \operatorname{erf}\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{\alpha t}}\right),\quad q_s''(t) = \frac{k(T_s - T_i)}{\sqrt{\pi\alpha t}}$$ $$\text{Constant } q_o'': T - T_i = \frac{2q_o''(\alpha t/\pi)^{1/2}}{k}\exp\left(\frac{-x^2}{4\alpha t}\right) - \frac{q_o''x}{k}\operatorname{erfc}\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{\alpha t}}\right)$$ $$\text{Surface convection: } \frac{T - T_i}{T_\infty - T_i} = \operatorname{erfc}\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{\alpha t}}\right) - \exp\left(\frac{hx}{k} + \frac{h^2\alpha t}{k^2}\right)\operatorname{erfc}\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{\alpha t}} + \frac{h\sqrt{\alpha t}}{k}\right)$$

Convection Basics (pp. 4–5)

Newton's law of cooling p. 4
$$q = \bar hA(T_s - T_\infty),\qquad q'' = h(T_s - T_\infty)$$
Dimensionless numbers p. 5
$$Re = \frac{\rho vL_s}{\mu},\qquad Nu = \frac{hL_s}{k_f},\qquad Pr = \frac{\nu}{\alpha},\qquad St = \frac{Nu}{Re\,Pr}$$

$k_f$ is the fluid's conductivity. Mixing it up with the solid's $k$ (the one in $Bi$) is the classic mistake.

Boundary layer definitions and Chilton–Colburn analogy p. 5
$$C_f = \frac{2\tau_s}{\rho u_\infty^2},\qquad \tau_s = \mu\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\Big|_{y=0},\qquad h = \frac{-k_f\,\partial T/\partial y|_{y=0}}{T_s - T_\infty},\qquad \frac{C_f}{2} = St\,Pr^{2/3} \equiv j_H$$

Use the analogy to get $h$ from a measured drag or friction force (or the reverse).

Flat Plate (pp. 5–6)

Plate definitions p. 5
$$Re_x = \frac{\rho u_\infty x}{\mu},\qquad Nu_x = \frac{h_xx}{k_f},\qquad \overline{Nu}_L = \frac{\bar h_LL}{k_f},\qquad Re_{x,c} = 5\times10^5$$
Laminar, constant $T_s$ ($Pr \ge 0.6$) p. 5
$$\delta = \frac{5x}{\sqrt{Re_x}},\quad C_{f,x} = 0.664Re_x^{-1/2},\quad \bar C_f = 1.328Re_L^{-1/2}$$ $$Nu_x = 0.332Re_x^{1/2}Pr^{1/3},\quad \overline{Nu}_L = 0.664Re_L^{1/2}Pr^{1/3}$$

$\bar h_L = 2h_L$ (the average is twice the trailing-edge value).

Turbulent, constant $T_s$ ($0.6 \le Pr \le 60$) pp. 5–6
$$\delta = 0.37xRe_x^{-1/5},\qquad C_{f,x} = 0.0592Re_x^{-1/5}\ (Re_x \le 10^7),\qquad Nu_x = 0.0296Re_x^{4/5}Pr^{1/3}$$ $$\text{Mixed } (Re_{x,c} = 5\times10^5): \overline{Nu}_L = \left(0.037Re_L^{4/5} - 871\right)Pr^{1/3},\qquad \bar C_{f,L} = 0.074Re_L^{-1/5} - \frac{1742}{Re_L}$$ $$\text{Fully turbulent: } \overline{Nu}_L = 0.037Re_L^{4/5}Pr^{1/3},\qquad \bar C_{f,L} = 0.074Re_L^{-1/5}\qquad (5\times10^5 \le Re_L \le 10^8)$$

"Fully turbulent" (trip wire at the leading edge) drops the $-871$. Fully turbulent $\bar h \propto L^{-1/5}$, so a shorter plate (in the flow direction) has the higher $\bar h$.

Constant surface heat flux p. 6
$$q'' = h_x(T_{s,x} - T_\infty),\qquad q = q''A = \bar h(\bar T_s - T_\infty)$$ $$Nu_x = 0.453Re_x^{1/2}Pr^{1/3}\ (\text{lam}),\qquad Nu_x = 0.0308Re_x^{4/5}Pr^{1/3}\ (\text{turb})$$ $$\overline{Nu}_L = 0.680Re_L^{1/2}Pr^{1/3}\ (\text{lam})$$

Turbulent average: use the constant-$T_s$ correlations. The hottest point is at the trailing edge (where $h_x$ is smallest).

Unheated starting length $\xi$ ($x$ measured from the leading edge) p. 6
$$Nu_x = \frac{Nu_x|_{\xi=0}}{\left[1 - (\xi/x)^{3/4}\right]^{1/3}}\ (\text{lam}),\qquad Nu_x = \frac{Nu_x|_{\xi=0}}{\left[1 - (\xi/x)^{9/10}\right]^{1/9}}\ (\text{turb})$$ $$\overline{Nu}_L = \overline{Nu}_L|_{\xi=0}\frac{L}{L - \xi}\left[1 - (\xi/L)^{(p+1)/(p+2)}\right]^{p/(p+1)},\qquad p = 2\ (\text{lam}),\ 8\ (\text{turb})$$

Apply $\bar h$ to the heated area only: $q = \bar h(L - \xi)W(T_s - T_\infty)$.

Flow around objects p. 7
$$\overline{Nu}_L = CRe_L^mPr^n\qquad \text{(}C, m, n\text{ and the length scale come from a table on the exam)}$$

For a cylinder at fixed velocity, $\bar h \propto D^{m-1}$ and $q' \propto D^m$.

Internal Flow (pp. 7–8)

Definitions and energy balance p. 7
$$D_h = \frac{4A_c}{P},\qquad Nu_D = \frac{hD}{k_f},\qquad \dot m = \rho u_mA_c,\qquad q = \dot mc_p(T_{m,o} - T_{m,i}),\qquad q'' = h(T_s - T_m)$$
Constant surface temperature p. 7
$$T_m(x) = T_s - (T_s - T_{m,i})\exp\left(-\frac{Px}{\dot mc_p}\bar h\right),$$ $$q = \bar hA_s\Delta T_{lm},\qquad \Delta T_{lm} = \frac{\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2}{\ln(\Delta T_1/\Delta T_2)},\qquad \Delta T_1 = T_s - T_{m,i},\ \Delta T_2 = T_s - T_{m,o}$$

Solving backwards for $\bar h$ from measured temperatures: $\bar h = \dfrac{\dot mc_p}{PL}\ln\dfrac{T_s - T_{m,i}}{T_s - T_{m,o}}$.

External fluid at $T_\infty$ instead of a fixed wall (corrected) p. 7
$$\frac{T_\infty - T_m(x)}{T_\infty - T_{m,i}} = \exp\left(-\frac{\bar UA_s}{\dot mc_p}\right),\qquad \frac{1}{\bar UA_s} = R_{tot} = \frac{1}{\bar h_iA_i} + R_{wall} + R_{ins} + \frac{1}{\bar h_oA_o}$$
Uniform surface heat flux not on sheet
$$T_m(x) = T_{m,i} + \frac{q_s''P}{\dot mc_p}x,\qquad T_s(x) = T_m(x) + \frac{q_s''}{h}$$
Laminar ($Re_D \lt 2300$) p. 8
$$Re_D = \frac{\rho u_mD}{\mu} = \frac{u_mD}{\nu} \left(= \frac{4\dot m}{\pi D\mu}\ \text{circular}\right),\qquad \frac{x_{fd}}{D} = 0.05Re_D,\qquad \frac{x_{fd,t}}{D} = 0.05Re_DPr$$ $$\text{Fully developed: } Nu_D = 4.36\ (\text{constant } q''),\qquad Nu_D = 3.66\ (\text{constant } T_s)$$ $$\text{Both developing: } \overline{Nu}_D = 1.86\left(\frac{Re_DPr}{L/D}\right)^{1/3}\left(\frac{\mu}{\mu_s}\right)^{0.14}$$ $$\text{Thermal entry only: } \overline{Nu}_D = 3.66 + \frac{0.0668(D/L)Re_DPr}{1 + 0.04[(D/L)Re_DPr]^{2/3}}$$

Fully developed laminar $h = Nu\,k/D$ doesn't depend on flow rate. That's how a measured $\bar h$ gives you $k_f$ (practice exam P4).

Turbulent p. 8
$$\frac{x_{fd}}{D} \approx \frac{x_{fd,t}}{D} \approx 10,\qquad Nu_D = 0.023Re_D^{4/5}Pr^n,\quad n = 0.4\ \text{fluid heating},\ 0.3\ \text{fluid cooling}$$

Noncircular: use $D_h$ in both $Re$ and $Nu$. Read the problem: the practice exam said "use $n = 0.4$" for everything.

Free and Mixed Convection (p. 9)

Groups ($\beta = 1/T$ for ideal gases, in kelvin) p. 9
$$Gr_L = \frac{g\beta(T_s - T_\infty)L^3}{\nu^2},\qquad Ra_L = Gr_LPr = \frac{g\beta(T_s - T_\infty)L^3}{\nu\alpha},\qquad Ra_{x,c} \approx 10^9$$
Vertical plate p. 9
$$\text{Similarity (laminar): } Nu_x = \left(\frac{Gr_x}{4}\right)^{1/4}g(Pr),\quad \overline{Nu}_L = \frac43\left(\frac{Gr_L}{4}\right)^{1/4}g(Pr)$$ $$g(Pr) = \frac{0.75Pr^{1/2}}{(0.609 + 1.221Pr^{1/2} + 1.238Pr)^{1/4}}$$ $$\text{Empirical: } \overline{Nu}_L = CRa_L^n:\quad \text{laminar } C = 0.59,\ n = \tfrac14;\quad \text{turbulent } C = 0.1,\ n = \tfrac13$$

Turbulent: $\overline{Nu} \propto L$, so $\bar h$ doesn't depend on height. Other geometries: "provided as necessary".

Combined free and forced p. 9
$$\frac{Gr_L}{Re_L^2} \ll 1:\ \text{forced}\qquad \frac{Gr_L}{Re_L^2} \gg 1:\ \text{free}\qquad \frac{Gr_L}{Re_L^2} \approx 1:\ Nu^3 = Nu_F^3 \pm Nu_N^3$$

$+$ when buoyancy and the forced flow go the same way (assisting), $-$ when opposed.

Heat Exchangers (pp. 9–10)

Overall coefficient pp. 9–10
$$\frac{1}{UA} = \frac{1}{U_hA_h} = \frac{1}{U_cA_c} = R_{eq},\qquad \text{clean, no fins: } \frac{1}{UA} = \frac{1}{(hA)_c} + R_{wall} + \frac{1}{(hA)_h}$$ $$\text{Fouling and fins: } \frac{1}{UA} = \frac{1}{(\eta_ohA)_c} + \frac{R''_{f,c}}{\eta_oA_c} + R_{wall} + \frac{R''_{f,h}}{\eta_oA_h} + \frac{1}{(\eta_ohA)_h}$$ $$\text{Concentric tube: } \frac{1}{UA} = \frac{1}{h_cA_c} + \frac{R''_{f,c}}{A_c} + \frac{\ln(r_o/r_i)}{2\pi kL} + \frac{R''_{f,h}}{A_h} + \frac{1}{h_hA_h}$$

Each $A$ is the area that fluid touches: $\pi D_{inner}L$ for the fluid in the inner pipe, $\pi D_{outer}L$ for the annulus side of that same wall. Since every term has $1/L$, solving for $L$ is one division.

Annulus geometry p. 10
$$D_h = D_o - D_i,\qquad A_c = \frac{\pi D_o^2}{4} - \frac{\pi D_i^2}{4}$$
Energy balances and LMTD (corrected subscript) p. 10
$$q = \dot m_hc_{p,h}(T_{h,i} - T_{h,o}) = \dot m_cc_{p,c}(T_{c,o} - T_{c,i}),\qquad q = UA\Delta T_{lm},\qquad \Delta T_{lm} = \frac{\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2}{\ln(\Delta T_1/\Delta T_2)}$$

Counterflow: $\Delta T_1 = T_{h,i} - T_{c,o}$, $\Delta T_2 = T_{h,o} - T_{c,i}$. Parallel: $\Delta T_1 = T_{h,i} - T_{c,i}$, $\Delta T_2 = T_{h,o} - T_{c,o}$. Condensing or boiling side: that fluid is at constant temperature.

ε–NTU p. 10
$$C = \dot mc_p,\quad q_{max} = C_{min}(T_{h,i} - T_{c,i}),\quad \varepsilon = \frac{q}{q_{max}},$$ $$NTU = \frac{UA}{C_{min}},\quad C_r = \frac{C_{min}}{C_{max}},\quad \varepsilon = f(NTU, C_r)$$
ε–NTU relations ("see tables": expect them provided) not on sheet
$$\text{Parallel: } \varepsilon = \frac{1 - \exp[-NTU(1 + C_r)]}{1 + C_r}$$ $$\text{Counterflow: } \varepsilon = \frac{1 - \exp[-NTU(1 - C_r)]}{1 - C_r\exp[-NTU(1 - C_r)]}\ (C_r \lt 1)$$ $$\text{Counterflow, } C_r = 1: \varepsilon = \frac{NTU}{1 + NTU}\qquad \text{Any type, } C_r = 0: \varepsilon = 1 - e^{-NTU}$$ $$\text{Counterflow: } NTU = \frac{1}{C_r - 1}\ln\left(\frac{\varepsilon - 1}{\varepsilon C_r - 1}\right),\qquad NTU = \frac{\varepsilon}{1 - \varepsilon}\ (C_r = 1)$$

Shell-and-tube and cross-flow relations are on the Ch 11 page.

Radiation (p. 10)

Small surface in large surroundings p. 10
$$q = \epsilon\sigma A(T_s^4 - T_{sur}^4) = h_rA(T_s - T_{sur}),$$ $$h_r = \epsilon\sigma(T_s^2 + T_{sur}^2)(T_s + T_{sur}),\qquad \sigma = 5.67\times10^{-8}\ \text{W/m}^2\text{K}^4$$

Kelvin only. In air near room temperature, $h_r \approx 5$–$7$ W/m²·K, the same size as free convection.
